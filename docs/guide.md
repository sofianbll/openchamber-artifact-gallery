# A home for the things your conversations made

Artifact Gallery is a local OpenChamber extension for finding and reusing pages,
images, captures and deliverables. It lives inside the app, as a page or side panel.

![Artifact Gallery inside OpenChamber, showing fictional projects](assets/host-gallery.png)

## Your first minute

1. [Install from the repository URL](installation.md) in Settings → Extensions.
2. Open **Artifact Gallery** in the upper-left sidebar row, before search.
3. Start with **Session**, or select **All** to see results from other spaces.
4. Open an artifact, then choose **Add to message** to put a reference in your draft.

The gallery does not send a message. The draft stays in your active conversation,
even when you browse another project. Click the attached chip to reopen its artifact.

## What appears here?

Pages made with `render.page`, browser captures, explicitly opened deliverables,
assistant links to local files, and image/PDF attachments. Code patches and incidental
tool logs are excluded. Agents keep using their usual tools; there is no extra
publishing step.

The gallery is an index of sources, **not a backup**. Moving or deleting a source
can make its preview unavailable.
